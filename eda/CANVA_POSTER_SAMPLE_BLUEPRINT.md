## 1. Quick Start: Two Workflows to Create Your Poster in Canva

### Workflow A: Instant 1-Click Vector Import (Recommended & Fastest)
Canva has a built-in vector PDF ingestion engine that automatically converts publication-grade PDFs into fully editable Canva layouts with pre-placed text boxes, cards, and vector graphics:

1. Open your browser and navigate to [Canva.com](https://www.canva.com/).
2. On the top right of the Canva home page, click **"Upload"** &rarr; **"Choose files"** (or drag and drop directly onto the Canva home screen).
3. Select the pre-rendered publication-grade PDF file:  
   `C:\Users\manda\Downloads\DS311_A2_Research_Poster_Final.pdf`
4. Canva will parse the document and open it as a brand-new editable project titled **`DS311_A2_Research_Poster_Final`**.
5. Every single element (text box, card shape, table, and figure) will now be native, editable vector items directly on your Canva canvas!

---

### Workflow B: Manual Canvas Assembly from Scratch
If you or your group members prefer building the poster manually in Canva:

1. Click **"Create a design"** &rarr; **"Custom size"**.
2. Set unit to **`mm`** and enter:
   * **Width**: `420 mm`
   * **Height**: `594 mm`  
   *(Or in pixels at 300 DPI: `4961 x 7016 px`)*
3. Set Canvas Margins:
   * Go to **File** &rarr; **View settings** &rarr; **Show rulers and guides**.
   * Drag vertical guides at: `20 mm` (Left Margin), `135 mm`, `147 mm`, `292 mm`, `304 mm`, `400 mm` (Right Margin).
   * Drag horizontal guides at: `20 mm` (Top Margin), `100 mm` (Header End), `145 mm` (Takeaway Banner End), `560 mm` (Footer Start), `574 mm` (Bottom Margin).

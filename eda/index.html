CORE DISCOVERY
Across sovereign nations, every 1,000 m³/day surge in municipal wastewater generation yields only 296 m³/day in urban treatment expansion (β1 = 0.2964, R² = 82.0%) — creating an unabsorbed 70.4% structural infrastructure deficit that bypasses treatment.

01 Research Problem & Inquiry

SDG 6.3 & 11.6

Accelerated urbanization and demographic concentration drive exponential growth in municipal effluent. When centralized wastewater treatment plants (WWTPs) fail to scale proportionately, untreated discharges directly infiltrate receiving water bodies, triggering severe eutrophication, pathogen contamination, and macro-economic environmental damage.

The Business Invalidation Question: Does national urban treatment capacity expand at a 1:1 parity with municipal wastewater generation, or does an operational infrastructure gap widen — and what is the quantified capital expenditure (CAPEX) required to close it?

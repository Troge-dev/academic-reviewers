STRUCTURAL VERIFICATION
Word Count Drift
Why LLMs fail at exact word quotas and how to bound length reliably.
[ REVEAL TOKENOMICS ] ▸
SUB-WORD TOKENIZATION
Lack of Forward Counters

LLMs process text in sub-word tokens (~0.75 words/token), not natural words:

  • No Global Counter: During autoregressive decoding, models cannot look ahead or count total words generated.
  • Failure of Exact Quotas: Prompts demanding "exactly 100 words" routinely drift by 15–30%.
  • Engineering Remedy: Enforce asymmetric upper limits (e.g., "maximum 120 words") or discrete counts (e.g., "exactly 3 bullet points").

Negative Constraints
The attention paradox of "Do not mention" and lexical suppression.
[ REVEAL ATTENTION PARADOX ] ▸
ATTENTION VECTOR ACTIVATION
The Pink Elephant Paradox

Instructing an LLM "Do not say sorry" often causes it to apologize:

  • Attention Priming: Mentioning a banned word activates its embedding in the self-attention matrix, increasing related token probabilities.
  • RLHF Conversational Prior: Pre-trained models are heavily aligned to be polite, resisting negative lexical bans.
  • Verification Standard: Programmatic checks (b.lower() in text.lower()) prove whether the prompt successfully overrode default priors.

SEMANTIC INDUCTION
Prior Knowledge Mapping

In zero-shot prompting, the model relies on pre-trained semantic weights:

  • Minimal Overhead: Consumes only ~30–60 tokens per invocation.
  • Label Prior Skew: Prone to conversational verbosity (outputting "The category is urgent" instead of raw "urgent").
  • Boundary Vulnerability: Struggles when category boundaries are nuanced, overlapping, or subjective.

ZERO ENTROPY SAMPLING
Temperature = 0.0
Greedy argmax selection; collapses token probability distribution to determinism.
[ REVEAL MATHEMATICS ] ▸
GREEDY COLLAPSE
Argmax Probability Peak

Mathematically, as $T \to 0$, $P(w_i) \to 1.0$ for the highest logit:

  • 100% Deterministic: Running the prompt 5 times yields verbatim identical outputs.
  • Zero Creativity: Eliminates lexical exploration; locks the model into familiar clichés.
  • Mandatory For: Code compilation, SQL generation, JSON extraction, and classification.
[ RETURN TO FRONT ] ↺
HIGH ENTROPY STOCHASTICS
Temperature = 1.8
Flattens softmax distribution; activates the improbable tail of vocabulary.
[ REVEAL MATHEMATICS ] ▸
UNIFORM FLATTENING
Tail Exploration & Hallucination

Dividing logits by $T=1.8$ compresses differences between candidates:

  • High Surprise: Low-probability tokens are sampled, producing novel analogies and creative naming.
  • Instability Boundary: Approaching the uniform limit where syntax degrades into gibberish.
  • Target Use: Fiction, poetry, and exploratory brainstorming.

EMPATHETIC CADENCE
Logit Dominance
Why deterministic classifiers often refuse to flip even at T=1.8.
[ REVEAL INSIGHT ] ▸
MARGIN OF SEPARATION
Logit Gap $\Delta z$ Mechanics

If a classifier's answer never flipped at high temp:

  • It does not mean the model ignored temperature!
  • If the ground-truth label had logit $z=18$ and runner-up had $z=2$, even after $T=1.8$, $\exp(\Delta z/T)$ leaves the winner with >99% probability.
  • Only ambiguous edge cases flip.

DETERMINISTIC SYSTEMS

Explicit Handcoded Subroutines

Traditional computation relies on strict logic where every branch must be pre-programmed.

  • Mechanism: If X occurs, execute Y. Fails immediately if unhandled edge cases appear.
  • Generalization: Zero adaptation; cannot process ambiguous natural language or noisy data.
  • Cost Structure: High human authoring cost for every additional exception.

PROBABILISTIC INFERENCE

Learned Approximation Functions

  • Mechanism: Optimizes loss functions over empirical data distributions.
  • Generalization: Evaluates novel, unseen prompts by mapping inputs to semantic vector manifolds.
  • Resilience: Gracefully handles spelling errors, semantic nuance, and informal phrasing.

A Concise History of NLP & The Recurrent Bottleneck

The technological journey from brittle expert grammars to sequential RNNs and LSTM limitations

THE SEQUENTIAL DILEMMA

RNNs could not be trained in parallel because token t+1 strictly depended on the hidden state of token t. Long sentences caused gradients to vanish entirely.

THE PRE-TRANSFORMER BOTTLENECK (2010–2016)

RULE-BASED (1950s-1980s)

ELIZA & Hardcoded Trees

CLICK TO FLIP ↻

Handcrafted Grammars: Fragile rule trees capable of simple keyword substitution. Completely broke down when encountering slang, ambiguity, or typos.

STATISTICAL NLP (1990s-2000s)

N-Grams & Markov Chains

CLICK TO FLIP ↻

Frequency Counts: Counted word co-occurrences. Severely limited by combinatorial explosion and zero semantic abstraction.

RNNs & LSTMs (2010-2016)

Sequential Deep Learning

CLICK TO FLIP ↻

Vanishing Gradients: Backpropagating through time across hundreds of tokens caused gradients to vanish, forgetting text from earlier sentences.

The Transformer Revolution: Attention Is All You Need

How Vaswani et al. (2017) discarded recurrence to enable global self-attention across tokens

CORE MECHANISM

Scaled Dot-Product Self-Attention

Attention(Q, K, V) = Softmax(QKᵀ / √dₖ) V

CLICK OR TAB TO FLIP ↻
ATTENTION EQUATION

The Mathematics of Self-Attention

How queries, keys, and values compute universal semantic affinities.

  • Query (Q), Key (K), Value (V): Linear projections of token embeddings.
  • Dot Product (QKᵀ): Calculates similarity scores between every token pair simultaneously.
  • Scaling (√dₖ): Prevents dot products from growing excessively large, stabilizing Softmax gradients.

ARCHITECTURAL IMPACT

Total Parallelization & Context Retention

Why Transformers revolutionized machine learning

CLICK OR TAB TO FLIP ↻
GPU EFFICIENCY

Unlocking Internet-Scale Pre-Training

Overcoming the dual bottlenecks of sequential computation.

  • Full Parallelization: Entire sentences are processed concurrently; GPUs train at maximum throughput.
  • Global Receptive Field: Token 1 attends directly to Token 500 without passing through intermediary steps.
  • Multi-Head Attention: Enables models to attend to syntactic, semantic, and positional relationships simultaneously.

The basic atomic currency of language models

TOKENIZATION

How Text Becomes Numbers

Models do not read letters or whole words; they process subword tokens.

  • Subword Tokenization: Uses algorithms like Byte-Pair Encoding (BPE) or WordPiece.
  • Token Ratios: 1,000 tokens ≈ 750 English words (~4 characters per token).
  • Token Splitting: Uncommon words are split into pieces: "unbelievable" ➔ ["un", "believ", "able"].

Probabilistic Text Generation & Temperature Scaling

From raw output logits to Softmax probabilities: controlling determinism and entropy

SOFTMAX SCALING
P(wᵢ)

exp(zᵢ / T) / ∑ exp(zⱼ / T)

Temperature (T) acts as a divisor scaling logit scores before Softmax. T < 0.2 concentrates probability on the greedy top token; T > 0.8 spreads probability across diverse tokens.

LOGITS (RAW TENSOR SCORES)

Unnormalized Linear Layer Outputs

CLICK TO FLIP ↻

Logits: The final linear layer outputs raw scalar scores for every token in the vocabulary (~32k to 128k tokens). Softmax normalizes these into probabilities summing to 1.0.

LOW TEMPERATURE (T = 0.0 - 0.2)

Greedy, Factual, Deterministic

CLICK TO FLIP ↻

Factual Systems: Essential for RAG, math, and code generation. Eliminates creative deviation and forces the model to pick only the most probable factual tokens.

HIGH TEMPERATURE (T = 0.7 - 1.0)

Creative, Diverse, Stochastic

CLICK TO FLIP ↻

Creative Writing: Flattens the probability curve, allowing lower-ranked tokens to be sampled. Promotes creative brainstorming but drastically increases hallucination rates.

CONSUMER INTERFACES

General Knowledge with Privacy Risks

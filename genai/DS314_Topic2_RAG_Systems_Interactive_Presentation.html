Chunking Mechanics: Sizing & Boundary Overlap

Why embedding an entire PDF as one vector fails and how chunk_overlap preserves semantics

THE DILUTION PARADOX

If you embed an entire 50-page handbook into a single vector, pricing, history, mission, and refund rules blend into an uninformative mean vector.

VECTOR SEMANTIC CONCENTRATION PRINCIPLE

GRANULAR CHUNK SIZING

1,000 Character Target Sizing

CLICK TO FLIP ↻

Isolating Facts: Small chunks (~1,000 chars) isolate individual propositions (e.g., exact refund policy steps), allowing vector search to return the exact relevant paragraph.

THE BOUNDARY CLEAVING TRAP

Why Sentences Get Sliced in Half

CLICK TO FLIP ↻

Cleaving Trap: If an essential condition ("...unless requested after 30 days") falls exactly on a chunk edge, the logic is truncated. Overlap guarantees boundary continuity.

CHUNK OVERLAP (overlap=100)

10% Rolling Context Window

CLICK TO FLIP ↻

Rolling Buffer: The last 100 characters of Chunk 1 are copied to the beginning of Chunk 2, guaranteeing no sentence is cleaved without duplicate preservation.

SIMILARITY METRIC

Cosine Similarity Mathematics

cos(θ) = (q · d) / (||q|| ||d||)

CLICK OR TAB TO FLIP ↻
COSINE PROXIMITY

Measuring Directional Alignment

Why cosine similarity dominates natural language vector retrieval.

  • Formula: Measures the cosine of the angle between query vector q and chunk vector d.
  • Magnitude Invariance: Normalizes for vector length, meaning short queries match long chunks.
  • Score Scale: +1.0 = identical semantic meaning; 0.0 = orthogonal; -1.0 = opposite meaning.
  • Semantic Matching: "Where do I return items?" matches "Store refund policy" with high similarity.

Answer strictly based on the provided context. If the context does not contain the answer, explicitly state that the information is unavailable.

The Trap Question Mechanism

How evaluators test whether a RAG chatbot is truly grounded.

  • Concept: Ask a question that sounds completely plausible for the domain, but whose answer is missing from the document.

API TROUBLESHOOTING

Google Rate Limit Recovery

Overcoming Requests-Per-Minute (RPM) throttling during embedding.

  • Cause: Generating embeddings for 300+ chunks in a tight for-loop exceeds Google free-tier limits.
  • Remediation 1: Batch embedding calls into slices of 15 chunks with time.sleep(1) pauses.
  • Remediation 2: Test pipelines during development using document subsets (e.g. chunks[:10]).

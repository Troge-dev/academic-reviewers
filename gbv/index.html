EXECUTIVE CHAPTER 6

The Architecture of Gendered Violence

Gender-based violence is neither accidental nor interpersonal friction; it is the structural enforcement of systemic inequality.

Chapter 6 — Presenters: Mahilum (1) • Mandamian (2) • Marshburn (3) • Megalbio (4)

[ REVEAL MEANING ]

THREE DOMAINS Continuum of Harm
[ REVEAL MEANING ]

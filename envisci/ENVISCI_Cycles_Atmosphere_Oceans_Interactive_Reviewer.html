The Phosphorus Bottleneck & Sulfur Acidification

PHOSPHORUS TRAP

Zero Atmospheric Phase

Phosphorus cycles exclusively through rock weathering, soil assimilation, and deep marine sedimentation. No stable ambient gas exists.

The Geological Bottleneck

▸Phosphate rock (apatite) releases PO4 3- only through slow chemical weathering.
▸Primary limiting nutrient in freshwater: adding minute amounts triggers massive algal blooms.
▸Marine phosphate precipitates into deep abyss, trapped until tectonic uplift occurs epochs later.

Atmospheric Thermal Strata & Lapse Rates

The 5 concentric layers delineated strictly by temperature lapse rates, ozone photochemical heating, and kinetic excitation.

TROPOSPHERE (0 - 12 KM)

The Weather Engine

Temperature decreases with height (-6.5°C/km). Contains 75–80% of total atmospheric mass and virtually all water vapor. Capped by the Tropopause.

Click to reveal deep theory⟲
PHYSICAL DRIVER

Heated From Below

▸Ground absorbs solar light and radiates infrared heat upwards.
▸Pressure drops from ~1013 hPa at sea level to ~200 hPa at tropopause.
▸All convective clouds, typhoons, and storm dynamics occur here.
Click to flip back⟲
STRATOSPHERE (12 - 50 KM)

The Ozone Shield

Temperature increases with altitude (-60°C up to 0°C). Houses the Ozone Layer (O3), absorbing harmful solar UV radiation. Stable laminar airflow.

Click to reveal deep theory⟲
PHOTOCHEMICAL DRIVER

Chapman Ozone Cycle

▸O2 + UV-C -> 2O; O + O2 -> O3 + Heat. O3 absorbs UV-B, releasing heat.
▸Temperature inversion prevents vertical convection, making air extremely stable.
▸Commercial airliners cruise in lower stratosphere to avoid turbulence.

MESO & THERMOSPHERE

Cold Traps & Ionosphere

Mesosphere (50–85 km) drops to -90°C (coldest layer), burning meteors. Thermosphere (85–600 km) spikes to 1,500°C+, housing the Ionosphere and ISS.

Click to reveal deep theory⟲
UPPER ATMOSPHERE

Meteor Shield & Auroras

▸Mesosphere lacks ozone heating; CO2 radiative cooling drives temp to -90°C.
▸Meteors incinerate in mesosphere due to atmospheric friction and compression.
▸Thermosphere absorbs solar X-rays; ionized particles generate Auroras.

3-CELL ENGINE

Hadley, Ferrel & Polar

Pressure Belts & Deserts

▸Equatorial air rises at ITCZ (Low Pressure), sinking at 30°N/S (Horse Latitudes).

▸Polar front at 60° generates mid-latitude storm tracks and jet streams.

CORIOLIS VORTICITY

Inertial Deflection

Earth's eastward rotation deflects moving winds to the RIGHT in the Northern Hemisphere and to the LEFT in the Southern Hemisphere. Zero at equator.

Click to reveal deep theory⟲
PHYSICAL LAWS

Cyclone Spin Mechanics

▸Conserves angular momentum: surface velocity is 1670 km/h at equator, 0 at poles.
▸Deflects inward winds to right in NH, forcing cyclones into counterclockwise spin!
▸Because Coriolis force is zero at equator, typhoons cannot form within 5° of equator.
